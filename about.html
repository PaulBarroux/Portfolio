About 关于

I'm Paul Barroux, a UX/UI designer currently in my third year at L'École de Design Nantes Atlantique. My approach to design is rooted in understanding people — their needs, frustrations, and aspirations — and translating those insights into meaningful digital experiences. 我是 Paul Barroux，一名 UX/UI 设计师，目前就读于法国南特大西洋设计学院 (L'École de Design Nantes Atlantique) 三年级。我的设计方法源于对人的理解 — 他们的需求、痛点与愿望 — 并将这些洞察转化为有意义的数字体验。

I believe great design lives at the intersection of research and aesthetics. Every project I take on begins with deep listening: user interviews, contextual research, and careful analysis of the problem space. From there, I move through wireframing, prototyping, and iterative testing to arrive at solutions that are both functional and beautiful. 我相信优秀的设计存在于研究与美学的交汇处。每个项目都始于深度倾听：用户访谈、情境研究以及对问题的细致分析。在此基础上，我通过线框图、原型设计和迭代测试，最终得出兼具功能与美感的方案。

Beyond screens, I have a growing interest in the technical side of design. I work with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to prototype ideas, and I'm exploring 3D and motion design to push the boundaries of what digital experiences can feel like. 除了屏幕之外，我对设计的技术层面也越来越感兴趣。我使用 HTML、CSS 和 JavaScript 制作原型，同时探索 3D 与动效设计，以拓展数字体验的可能性。
